## Document Discrepancies (design vs. current code)

- **WaitUntil.** A lambda-polling stub with no behavioral tests; the design re-evaluates a predicate **subgraph** each
  poll (step 12).
- **Delegation.** `Gather`/`Choose` share Subgraph's `walkSubgraphChildren` directly rather than calling the `Subgraph`
  method (see the design doc's §10.1) — an intentional interim until a child-activation affordance exists.

## Outstanding / open questions

Tracked in the design doc's §10:

1. A framework affordance to derive a child activation, so combinators call the `Subgraph` method and `Subgraph` itself
   unifies onto the stamped-stack shape.
2. A public-API pause-mid-combinator resume test (needs mid-run pause injection; lands with the eventing API, step 33).
3. Realizing WaitUntil as a re-evaluated predicate subgraph with behavioral tests (step 12) — candidate: a guarded
   back-edge loop in topology (step 10's anticipated-extension note).
