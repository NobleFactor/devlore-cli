## Document Discrepancies (design vs. current code)

- **Delegation.** `Gather`/`Choose`/`WaitUntil` share Subgraph's `walkSubgraphChildren` directly rather than calling
  the `Subgraph` method (see the design doc's §10.1) — an intentional interim until a child-activation affordance
  exists.

## Outstanding / open questions

Tracked in the design doc's §10:

1. A framework affordance to derive a child activation, so combinators call the `Subgraph` method and `Subgraph` itself
   unifies onto the stamped-stack shape.
2. A public-API pause-mid-combinator resume test (needs mid-run pause injection; lands with the eventing API, step 33).
3. WaitUntil's remaining test-matrix rows (step 12): match-after-N-polls needs a fixture-level mutable probe;
   context-cancel and body-error fixtures are unwritten.
