# Status: Reconciliation

**Architecture document:** [5.1-reconciliation.md](5.1-reconciliation.md)

**State:** rewritten 2026-07-22 (phase-8 step 51, slice 4). The previous body was the issue-#156 draft plan; its
proposed machinery — the 4-value `Do`, `Reconcile<X>` triangle, `ExecutionEvent` / `EventSink` / `AuditLedger` /
`ReconciliationStore`, the stack-level reconcile-before-undo gate (`ErrDrifted`) — **did not land** (tree-verified:
zero hits; even the pre-rewrite completion table's "ErrDrifted Complete" row no longer matched the tree). The three
concerns were realized by other means, now the document's body: receipts + the trace as audit, `writ reconcile` over
the run index + recorded content identity as reconciliation (steps 47–48; `writ reconcile` and `internal/writ` are
gone), and the distributed recovery-safety gates (receipt precision, the recovery-digest tamper check, the
state-checked `ResumeUnwind`). The proposal is preserved as an explicitly unimplemented appendix, with the
where-its-ideas-went mapping (the `EventSink` idea became the 2.8 observability design). **Revised 2026-09-22
(#913):** the header note and "Relationship to the Writ Lifecycle" state the ruling that the four lifecycle
operations are defined by what each does to the record, the lifetime model (a deploy opens one; its receipts
stack and fold; a deploy replaces it, a decommission removes it; pruning is store housekeeping, undesigned), and
what `readback.Fold` does today against it.
